# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small business owners and staff preparing priced documents for customers. They need to enter items, review discounts and tax, and find prior documents while working through routine quoting or invoicing tasks.

## Product Purpose

Create generic priced documents, calculate trustworthy totals, and summarize activity by issue date. Success means a user can prepare a document, understand its totals, finalize it, and retrieve it later without having to reconcile the arithmetic by hand.

## Brand Personality

Simple, precise, composed. Swiss editorial cues should support clear hierarchy and careful alignment while keeping the interface familiar as a work tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative analytics, crowded card grids, heavy color, and playful controls that slow document entry. No specific external visual reference was supplied; these boundaries interpret the user's requested direction.

## Design Principles

- Put document editing and arithmetic first.
- Keep status and editability obvious.
- Make amounts and their derivation easy to inspect.
- Keep repeated work fast, including filtering, duplicating, and printing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target was requested. Use readable text and familiar controls as part of basic usability.
